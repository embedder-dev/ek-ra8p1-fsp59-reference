## Fixed defects

- **Production:** r019 measured 57.250 ms instead of 20 ms because generated GPT configuration did not select PCLKD when dedicated GPTCLK was disabled. r020 corrects the selection in the [RA8P1 BSP MDF, line 1318](../../pack_sources/ra8p1/Renesas.RA_mcu_ra8p1.5.9.0/.module_descriptions/Renesas%23%23BSP%23%23ra8p1%23%23fsp%23%23%23%235.9.0.xml#L1318). The AD3 retest passed.
- **Production:** r020 intermittently booted with `SCKDIVCR=0x10000000` instead of `0x00001110`, or faulted before PDM initialization despite matching firmware readback. A [full-speed fault trace](evidence/r020-unpaused-bus-fault.log) identified Rev. A (`MCUVER=0x01`); the [pre-transition trace](evidence/r020-pre-vscr-cache-state.log) showed both CM85 caches enabled and active when startup was about to request voltage scaling, against the Rev. A requirement in hardware manual Table 1.1 and §11.7. [r021 `bsp_clocks.c`](../../baseline/ra/fsp/src/bsp/mcu/all/bsp_clocks.c#L2987) disables and deactivates the CM85 caches around the type-6 startup transition, then restores the prior state. The reset matrix above passed without pausing startup. [Audited ELF placements](evidence/r021-startup-placements.log) put startup code in code MRAM and the reset stack in system SRAM on all three r021 bench images. This establishes the fix on the tested Rev. A XTAL configurations, not across every clock profile.
- **Fixture only:** The first analog run overflowed the 1 KiB stack. A generated 4 KiB stack resolved it; no DAC or ADC driver change was needed. The MRAM preflight initially compared reserved `MREZS` bits; the [fixture now masks the two documented flags](bench_mram.c#L65). Both refusals before the MRAM retest occurred before any scratch writes.

## Open items and validation limits

- The AD3 remained input-only on the four marker pins. DAC/ADC and scratch MRAM functional passes are from r020, not fresh r021 executions. This bench does not independently qualify analog voltage accuracy, PDM electrical timing or calibrated audio, continuous streaming, TrustZone, irreversible protection, or PLL/high-speed clock transitions. One Rev. A board at ambient conditions is not a production reliability sample.
- The [isolated board check](evidence/r021-board-check-direct-host.log) passed two GNU builds (no compiler warnings or configurator problems) and found zero unknown IDs among 551 board-profile pin settings. It separately reported 16 driver-to-pin-model component warnings, two per checked pin model: `r_acmphs` expects `cmp0` or `acmphs<channel>` and `r_ioport` expects `debug0`, `trace0`, or `cgc0`. Those driver binding paths were not exercised by the bench and require disposition; the supplied pin models were not modified. Sibling component and signal differences are recorded as informational in the same log.

## Release gates

- **Passed — bounded bench qualification.** r021 passed the reset and concurrent MIC1 checks in the table. The watchdog check was standalone only. Cold power-on was checked by post-reconnect UART/register responses because the on-board VCOM re-enumerated after the startup banner. Transfer and memory limits were enforced with application-only S-records; [r021 boundary checks](evidence/r021-flash-boundary-checks.log) record unchanged sampled option words and scratch bytes across downloads.
- **Passed — focused host and build gates.** [32 fixture checks](evidence/r021-fixture-tests.log), [four focused startup/CGC regressions](evidence/r021-clock-focused.log), and [both ARM core builds of the complete startup-clock translation unit with parameter checking on and off](evidence/r021-clock-arm-matrix.log) passed. Fresh [GNU](evidence/r021-board-check-direct-host.log) and [LLVM 21.1.1](evidence/r021-llvm-board-builds.log) minimal board projects built for CM85 and CM33 with zero warnings and configurator problems; the [startup source bytes matched](evidence/r021-board-build-hashes.log) on all four. LLVM artifacts were not flashed. The r020 → r021 clock change also produced [identical preprocessed tokens and ARM object bytes](evidence/r021-legacy-clock-parity.log) on generated EK-RA8M1 and EK-RA6M5 controls, with parameter checking on and off. That comparison covers this clock change, not full 5.9.0 stock parity.
- **Open — full host regression.** The restored FSP 5.9.0 ZIP matches [recorded provenance](../../reference/provenance.json); the exact stock baseline revision, 3,135 source files, and 1,019 MDFs verified in an isolated worktree. The [broad GNU 13.2.1 run](evidence/host-suite-recovered.log#L1375) used a snapshot made before r021 and did not pass: 816 tests, 17 failures, 20 errors, and 12 skips. The 20 errors and 13 failures came from missing test inputs; the four other failed MDF assertions passed when rerun unchanged against authentic original MDFs. The [focused r021 follow-up](evidence/host-suite-recovered.log#L1424) passed all 41 tests with verified pack, memory-contract, SVD, and frozen stock inputs; it did not replace the broad run. Restore the remaining [original generated controls, historical pack and fixture revisions, and validation specs/apps](evidence/host-suite-recovered.log#L1529) before a full rerun against the exact r021 release tree. The retained CPU1 compile database matches its recorded hash, but its generated family header does not; that fixture was not substituted.
- **Open — production coverage.** Close or disposition the remaining host and pin-binding findings, rerun DAC/ADC and bounded MRAM on the release image, and validate the intended PLL/clock profiles, production toolchains and reset behavior over voltage, temperature, board and silicon-revision samples. Establish application-specific soak, negative-case and recovery gates before release.
