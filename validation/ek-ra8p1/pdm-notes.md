# EK-RA8P1 onboard PDM smoke test

This captures **MIC1 only**. The EK-RA8P1 v1 User’s Manual (R20UT5309EG0104, §6.5/table 31) routes the two SPH0690LM4H-1 microphones to P812 (clock) and P502 (data), with MIC1 SELECT low. The microphone datasheet (Rev A, table 7) specifies rising-edge latching for SELECT low. The unchanged pin model assigns these to `p812.pdm2.pdmclk2` and `p502.pdm2.pdmdat2`; the board already selects these routes. They do not overlap SCI8 VCOM (PD02/PD03), AD3 (P514/P111/P806/P807), or the analog loopback (P014/P001). No rewiring is needed.

The RA8P1 hardware manual (R01UH1064EJ0130, §9.10.34 and §50.3.1) specifies fixed MOCO for PDMIFCLK and a 1–62.5 MHz PCLKB. Keep the 24 MHz XTAL base and 12 MHz PCLKB; do not change SCI8 clocks. Verify MOCO is running (`MOCOCR.MCSTP=0`). Divider `/4` gives a nominal 2 MHz microphone clock (inside the microphone’s 1.1–4.8 MHz normal range), and sinc decimation 62 with half-band `/2` gives approximately 16.13 ksample/s. The r020 HAL adds 35 ms of microphone startup/wake time (Knowles datasheet p. 2) to its own filter settling delay.

## Integration and acceptance

1. From the repository root, copy `validation/ek-ra8p1/bench_pdm.c` into the active r020 CPU0 project’s `src/`. In its existing `bench.c`, declare `bool bench_pdm(void);` and add a foreground UART command branch: `else if (0 == strcmp(line, "pdm")) { ok = select_marker(-1) && bench_pdm(); }`. Do not call it from the UART ISR or at boot. Leave PDM and its interrupts unclaimed by CPU1.
2. Apply `embedder e2 config set <CPU0-project> --spec validation/ek-ra8p1/cpu0-pdm.json`. Check `embedder e2 problems <CPU0-project>`; verify generated `g_bench_pdm`, both PDM2 vectors (`pdm_dat_isr`/`pdm_err_isr`) at one priority, both PDM pin entries, and the unchanged 4 KiB stack. Run `embedder e2 build <CPU0-project> --rebuild` and use the existing image-audit/flash safety gates.
3. With SCI8 at 115200 baud, send `pdm` plus LF once per capture, first in quiet and then with a sustained tone or voice near MIC1. Expect `samples=2048 complete=1 errors=0 overruns=0 flags=0x00000000 timeout=0`, signed PCM statistics with different min/max, `PDM PASS`, and command `ACK pdm`. Compare `mean_abs`/range between quiet and stimulus and repeat to check reopen. An overrun, other error, timeout, constant or entirely railed data, or failed cleanup is not a pass. AD3 does not currently observe the PDM signals; the UART statistics alone do not verify audio content or clock timing.
